Rewrites the loaded program for a drag knife: the spindle runs ahead of the blade tip by the blade offset, swings around each sharp corner so the tip turns in place, and ramps into each cut so the blade lines up before full depth. The program should describe the cut you want, with Z0 on the top of the material.
